## 🚨 RED FLAGS - AVOID THESE

### Never Buy Domains With:
- ❌ Casino, adult, or pharma backlinks
- ❌ Chinese/Russian spam links
- ❌ PBN (Private Blog Network) history
- ❌ Sudden traffic drops (penalty indicator)
- ❌ Not indexed in Google
- ❌ Same anchor text > 50% of links
- ❌ Recent registration (under 2 years)

### Check Wayback for:
- Spam content
- Redirect chains
- Adult content
- Foreign language spam
- Sudden content changes
