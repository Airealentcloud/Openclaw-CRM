## 🔎 STEP 3: DOMAIN ANALYSIS CHECKLIST

### A. Wayback Machine Analysis
**URL:** https://web.archive.org/

**Check:**
- ✅ Was it a REAL business/website?
- ✅ Content relevance to real estate/Nigeria?
- ✅ Consistent history (no sudden changes)?
- ❌ No spam, PBN, or adult content
- ❌ No sudden traffic drop (penalty indicator)

### B. Backlink Profile Analysis
**Tools:** Ahrefs, Majestic, Moz

**Red Flags:**
- Casino, adult, pharma links
- Foreign language links (Russian, Chinese spam)
- Sudden backlink spikes
- Same anchor text over-optimization
- Link farms/PBN networks

**Green Flags:**
- Nigerian news sites (Vanguard, Punch, Guardian)
- International media (BBC, CNN mentioning Nigeria)
- .gov.ng or .edu.ng links
- Real estate directories
- Natural anchor text distribution

### C. Google Index Check
**Command:** `site:domainname.com`

**Results:**
- ✅ Indexed pages = Active in Google
- ⚠️ No results = May be penalized or never indexed
- ❌ "Did you mean" = Possible spam history

### Avoid These Domain Types:
1. **Previously Used for Spam** - Check Wayback thoroughly
2. **Chinese/Russian Link Profiles** - Usually low quality
3. **Exact Match Penalty Risk** - Over-optimized anchor text
