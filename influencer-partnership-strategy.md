# Influencer Partnership Strategy for A.I Realent
## Commission-Based Real Estate Marketing Through Nigerian Content Creators

**Strategy Name:** A.I Realent Creator Partners
**Launch Date:** March 2026
**Target:** Nigerian real estate micro-influencers on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Instead of spending on ads, partner with content creators who already have your target audience. Pay only when they bring closed deals.

**Why This Works:**
- Zero upfront cost
- Influencers have built-in trust with followers
- Content lives forever (vs. one-time ads)
- Scalable — add more partners as you grow

---

## 📊 Influencer Landscape Research

### Nigerian Real Estate Influencer Categories

**Tier 1: Mega Influencers (500K+ followers)**
- Cost: Too expensive for commission-only
- Verdict: Skip for now

**Tier 2: Macro Influencers (100K-500K followers)**
- Examples: @the_real_estate_guy, @propertyinvestorng
- Challenge: Expect upfront payment
- Verdict: Approach later with hybrid model

**Tier 3: Micro Influencers (10K-100K followers)** — **YOUR TARGET**
- 12+ identified in real estate niche
- Hungry for monetization
- Willing to work commission-only
- Higher engagement rates (3-8% vs. 1-2% for mega)

**Tier 4: Nano Influencers (1K-10K followers)**
- Very high engagement
- Limited reach
- Verdict: Test with 2-3 as experiment

## 🎯 Target Influencer Profiles

### Ideal Partner Characteristics

**Demographics:**
- Age: 25-40 years old
- Location: Lagos, Abuja, or UK/US (diaspora audience)
- Followers: 10,000 - 100,000
- Engagement rate: 3%+
- Posting frequency: At least 3x per week

**Content Style:**
- Real estate focused OR
- Business/wealth building OR
- Diaspora lifestyle OR
- Nigerian lifestyle/culture

**Audience:**
- Age 25-45 (prime property buying age)
- Location: Nigeria + diaspora (UK, US, Canada)
- Interest: Investment, property, wealth building

**Red Flags (Avoid):**
- Fake followers (check engagement quality)
- Controversial content
- Inconsistent posting
- No clear niche

## 📋 Influencer Onboarding Process

### Step 1: Identification (Week 1)

**Tools to Find Influencers:**
1. **FindMeCreators.com** — Filter by Nigerian real estate
2. **HiveInfluence.io** — TikTok real estate niche
3. **StarNgage.com** — Entrepreneur category
4. **Manual search:** #AbujaRealEstate #NigeriaProperty hashtags

**Create Target List:**
- 20 micro-influencers (10K-100K)
- 5 nano-influencers (1K-10K) for testing
- Prioritize those with diaspora audience

### Step 2: Outreach (Week 2)

**DM Template:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> I've been following your content on [platform] — your insights on [specific topic] really stand out.
>
> I'm Israel from A.I Realent, a verified real estate company in Abuja. We help Nigerians buy genuine properties with full documentation.
>
> I'm looking for content creators to partner with on a commission basis — no upfront cost to you.
>
> **The deal:**
> - You showcase our verified properties
> - Your followers mention your code when they buy
> - You earn 1.5-2.5% commission per closed deal
> - Typical earnings: ₦150K-₦500K per sale
>
> Interested in a 15-minute call to discuss?
>
> Best,
> Israel
> A.I Realent

**Email Template (for those with business emails):**

> Subject: Partnership Opportunity — Real Estate Commission Program
>
> [Same content as DM]

### Step 3: Vetting (Week 3)

**Check Before Partnership:**
- [ ] Engagement rate (use online calculators)
- [ ] Follower quality (check comments for bots)
- [ ] Content consistency
- [ ] Audience demographics (ask for insights screenshot)
- [ ] Previous brand partnerships
- [ ] Professionalism in communication

### Step 4: Agreement (Week 4)

**Influencer Agreement Must Include:**
- Commission structure
- Payment terms (14 days after deal closes)
- Content requirements (minimum posts per month)
- Exclusivity (can they work with other real estate companies?)
- Termination clause

### Step 5: Content Briefing (Week 5)

**Provide Influencers:**
- Property photos/videos
- Key selling points
- Your unique angle (AI verification)
- Sample scripts/captions
- Hashtag suggestions
- Tracking code for their referrals

## 🎬 Content Strategy for Partners

### Content Types That Convert

**1. Property Tour Videos**
- Format: 60-90 seconds
- Hook: "This ₦80M Abuja property has a feature I've never seen..."
- CTA: "DM me 'PROPERTY' for details"

**2. "Day in the Life" Investment Stories**
- Format: 3-5 minute YouTube/IGTV
- Content: Follow a buyer from search to purchase
- Your role: The verification expert

**3. Myth-Busting Content**
- "3 lies Abuja estate agents tell"
- "How to spot a fake C of O"
- Position you as the honest alternative

**4. Before/After Investment Stories**
- Client bought at ₦50M, now worth ₦120M
- Social proof + FOMO

**5. Live Q&A Sessions**
- Monthly Instagram Live with you
- Influencer hosts, you answer property questions
- Builds trust in real-time

### Content Calendar Per Influencer

**Month 1: Introduction**
- Week 1: Introduce partnership
- Week 2: First property feature
- Week 3: Educational content (verification importance)
- Week 4: Client testimonial

**Month 2+: Consistency**
- 2 property features per week
- 1 educational post per week
- 1 behind-the-scenes per week

## 🔗 Tracking & Attribution

### How to Track Influencer Referrals

**Method 1: Unique Promo Codes**
- Each influencer gets code: AIREALENT-[NAME]
- Client mentions code when they contact you
- Simple, effective

**Method 2: Unique Landing Pages**
- airealent.ng/influencer/[name]
- Track visits and form submissions
- More data, requires setup

**Method 3: WhatsApp Direct Links**
- wa.me/[number]?text=Hi, [InfluencerName] sent me
- Auto-populates message with influencer name
- Best for Nigerian market

**Recommended: Combine Method 1 + 3**

## 💡 Pro Tips for Success

### Do:
- Let influencers be authentic — don't over-script
- Provide high-quality visuals they can use
- Pay commissions promptly (builds trust)
- Share success stories with all partners (motivates them)
- Give exclusive property previews to top performers

### Don't:
- Micromanage content creation
- Delay commission payments
- Work with influencers whose values don't align
- Expect immediate results (takes 2-3 months to build)
- Ignore low-performing partners (help them improve first)
