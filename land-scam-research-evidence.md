## 🚩 COMMON SCAM TACTICS (Documented)

### The 8 Lies Scammers Tell (Omonile Lawyer Research)
1. "The land no get problem"
2. "Na our family land"
3. "The land get C of O or Gazette"
4. "Na you go be the first person for the land no worry"
5. "Excision in process" (Lagos no longer issues excisions!)
6. "Governor's Consent is being processed"
7. "Buy now before price increases"
8. "This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity"

### 25 Warning Signs of Fraudulent Real Estate Companies
**Top Red Flags:**
1. Owner roams with mobile police (hiding from victims)
2. No physical office address
3. Pressure to pay quickly
4. Price 30-50% below market
5. Reluctance to show original documents
6. "Excision in process" claims
7. Can't meet at actual property
8. Fake Governor's Consent documents
9. Multiple companies with same directors
10. No verifiable track record

## 🔍 HOW TO VERIFY (Backed by Evidence)

### The 5-Step Verification Process
1. **AGIS Check** — Every legitimate C of O in Abuja is registered
2. **Survey Plan Match** — Cross-check beacon numbers at Land Registry
3. **Physical Inspection** — Visit with licensed surveyor
4. **Seller Identity** — Verify ID matches land records
5. **Legal Review** — Lawyer reviews all documents

### Document Red Flags (From Real Cases)
- **Fake C of O:** Forged watermarks, altered dates, mismatched fonts
- **Fake Governor's Consent:** Thousands circulating in Lagos
- **Fake Survey Plans:** Fraudulent surveyors creating fake beacon numbers
- **Double Sales:** Same land sold to multiple buyers

### Technology Solutions
- **AI Document Analysis:** 94% accuracy in detecting forged documents
- **AGIS Database:** Official government records for verification
- **QR Code Verification:** Instant property authentication
