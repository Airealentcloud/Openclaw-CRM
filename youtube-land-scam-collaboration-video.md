## 🎬 VIDEO STRUCTURE

### **Hook (0:00 - 0:45)**
**Visual:** Dramatic reenactment of a land scam in progress
**Audio:** Tense music, phone call recording (actor)
**Script:**
> "In 2024, a Lagos businessman lost ₦45 million to a land scam in Abuja. The documents looked real. The agent seemed professional. The property... didn't exist. Today, we're pulling back the curtain on how land scams work—and showing you the technology that's making them obsolete."

**Visual Elements:**
- News headlines about land scams
- Quick cuts of fake documents
- Victim interview (silhouette)
- Transition to your face

---

### **Part 1: The Anatomy of a Land Scam (0:45 - 3:30)**

#### **Scene 1: The Setup (0:45 - 1:45)**
**Visual:** Split screen showing scammer's process
**You:**> "Land scammers follow a playbook. First, they identify a real property—often one that's not for sale. Then they create fake documents that look identical to the real thing."

**Graphics:**
- Flowchart: Identify Property → Forge Documents → Find Buyer → Disappear
- Side-by-side: Real C of O vs. Fake C of O (highlight differences)

#### **Scene 2: The Red Flags (1:45 - 2:45)**
**Visual:** You at a desk with documents
**Script:**> "Here are 5 red flags we see in 90% of land scams:"

**List with B-roll:**
1. Price 30-50% below market (show comparable prices)
2. Pressure to pay quickly (recreated pressure tactics)
3. Reluctance to show original documents
4. Agent can't meet at the actual property
5. No verifiable office address

#### **Scene 3: Real Case Study (2:45 - 3:30)**
**Visual:** Interview with AGIS official
**AGIS Representative:**> "Last year, we identified over 200 fake C of O documents. The forgeries are getting sophisticated—scanners, watermarks, even fake signatures. But there's one thing they can't fake..."

**Tease:** Transition to technology solution

### **Part 2: The Technology Solution (3:30 - 8:00)**

#### **Scene 4: AGIS Verification System (3:30 - 5:00)**
**Visual:** Tour of AGIS office, computer screens
**AGIS Official:**> "Every legitimate C of O in Abuja is registered in our database. When you verify through AGIS, you're checking against the official government record—not the paper someone handed you."

**Demo:**
- Screen recording: AGIS verification portal
- Enter plot number → Database search → Official record appears
- Compare with fake document

**Your Commentary:**> "This is the first layer of protection. But we're adding something even more powerful..."

#### **Scene 5: AI Document Analysis (5:00 - 6:30)**
**Visual:** Tech company office, developers at work

**Demo:**
- Upload document to AI system
- AI highlights suspicious areas in red
- Confidence score: "87% probability of forgery"

**Graphics:**
- Before/After: Human eye vs. AI detection
- Statistics: "94% accuracy in detecting forged documents"

#### **Scene 6: Developer Partnership (6:30 - 8:00)**

**Developer:**> "As developers, we've seen buyers lose millions. Now we're partnering with A.I Realent to verify every property before it hits the market. Buyers get peace of mind. We get faster sales."

**B-roll:**
- Verified properties with QR codes
- Buyers scanning codes at site
- Instant verification on phone

**Your Role:**> "This is the future—verified properties, transparent processes, zero scams."

### **Part 3: How to Protect Yourself (8:00 - 11:00)**

#### **Scene 7: The Verification Checklist (8:00 - 9:30)**
**Visual:** You at whiteboard, animated graphics
**Script:**> "Before you buy any land in Abuja, follow this 5-step verification process:"

**Animated Checklist:**
1. **AGIS Check** — Verify C of O at agis.gov.ng
2. **Survey Plan Match** — Cross-check beacon numbers
3. **Physical Inspection** — Visit with licensed surveyor
4. **Seller Identity** — Verify ID matches land records
5. **Legal Review** — Have a lawyer review all documents

**Each step:**
- Icon animation
- 10-second explanation
- Real example

#### **Scene 8: Live Verification Demo (9:30 - 11:00)**
**Visual:** You at an actual property
**Script:**> "Let's verify this property together in real-time."

**Live Process:**
1. Show property exterior
2. Log into AGIS portal on phone
3. Enter plot number
4. Show matching record
5. Check survey plan
6. Confirm with on-site beacon

**Result:**> "Verified. This property is legitimate."
